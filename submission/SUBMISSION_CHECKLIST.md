# Unstop submission checklist

1. Open `Top10_Batters.csv` and enter the players in rank order in the Unstop batter dropdowns.
2. Open `Top10_Bowlers.csv` and enter the players in rank order in the Unstop bowler dropdowns.
3. Upload `Royal_Intelligence_Methodology_Report.pdf` in the methodology report field.
4. Use the dashboard URL as supporting material only if the hackathon submission allows an additional link.
5. Before final submission, verify the official auction registration list, overseas availability/NOCs and all opt-outs. The supplied roster lists are not the official auction register.
