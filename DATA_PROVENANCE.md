# Data provenance

The analytics are based on the user-provided Round 1 dataset pack:
- `ball_by_ball.csv`
- `matches.csv`
- `competitions.csv`
- `competition_team_player_mapping.csv`

The static website bundles derived aggregate rankings only. Raw delivery-level records are not copied into `public/`.

Competition scope: IPL seasons 2022–2026 and men's T20 Internationals from 2022 onward. Other T20 competitions are excluded.
